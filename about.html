Studio Manifesto & people Est. MMXIV

We work the way we publish — slowly.

Carrara is a small studio of image, word and object working beside a journal of the same name. We make identities, publications and spaces for institutions and houses that would rather be remembered than merely seen — and we make them with a conviction that has become unfashionable: less, but better.

Over a decade we have held the same twelve accounts and printed every page ourselves. No growth for growth's sake; no portfolio built from noise. Our measure of a good year is how many of our decisions we would make again — untouched, unrevised.

Principles

Four refusals

What we decline, by habit
01

We refuse to fill space.

Whitespace is not a placeholder awaiting content. It is the content's memory of the essay, the margin's argument for the text. If a page would survive the removal of a sentence, we remove it.

02

We refuse to be urgent.

Trends are the weather; craft is the climate. We decline briefs that mistake speed for attention and metrics for meaning — and we have declined many, politely, in the same handwriting.

03

We refuse the obvious palette.

A canvas of warm ivory will not be noise. Our work is expected to whisper. When a restraint goes unmentioned, it has succeeded.

04

We refuse to dismiss the machine.

Automation is a colleague, not a rival. We set type by machine and choose it by eye, letting each tool keep the work it does best — and we keep, always, the hesitation.
